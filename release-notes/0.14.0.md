## Seneca 0.14.0

- **A clearer desktop.** The app uses a shared ivory, gold, and teal palette, with Oxanium titles and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for buttons and reading.
- **More room to study.** Mainframe rearranges its panels on narrow displays. Workspace keeps the draft you already started when opening a different study topic. The companion returns to its last saved position.
- **Plan from the timetable.** School Day marks today and the current time. Double-click an empty slot to add a class or schedule a study block for an assignment.

Open **Help & About → Updates** to find the right build. An installed Windows copy can apply the update in place. For portable Windows, extract the full ZIP into a new folder. On macOS 14 or later, choose the Apple Silicon (`arm64`) or Intel (`x86_64`) ZIP, quit Seneca, and replace the app in Applications.

Study work, drafts, preferences, and saved connections remain in the protected profile. A new Mac build may ask for Keychain access again. The Windows build is unsigned and the Mac builds use ad-hoc signing.
