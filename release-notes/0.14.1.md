## Seneca 0.14.1

- **One Home window.** Open Today, Classes, Chat, or Calendar from Seneca’s right-click menu. Home remembers its size and position.
- **Classes and notes together.** Each class has its overview, notes, files, assignments, and practice. Linked notebook notes keep their original links and revisions.
- **Chat with context.** Choose a class and see the sources attached to the conversation. Switching classes keeps other drafts saved, and the composer stays visible on short displays.
- **One focus timer.** Move between views during a study block. The weekly Calendar shows assignments and opens a prefilled form when you click an empty slot.

Open **Help & About → Updates → Check for updates now**. Installed Windows copies can update in place. Portable Windows users can extract the complete ZIP into a new folder. On macOS 14 or later, choose the ZIP for your Mac, quit Seneca, and replace the app in Applications.

Study work, drafts, preferences, and saved connections stay in your protected profile. The Windows installer passed an upgrade from 0.14.0, and both Mac builds passed app-replacement checks. Windows uses an unsigned installer, and the Mac builds use ad-hoc signing.
